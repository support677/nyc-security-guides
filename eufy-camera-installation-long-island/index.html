The farthest camera drains first

A battery camera at the edge of its range works harder to stay connected, and that extra radio work shows up as battery drain. Owners in the eufy forums describe exactly this pattern: the camera farthest from the HomeBase dies in a few days while the others last much longer. On Island lots the classic version is a detached garage at the end of the driveway, separated by open air and a couple of exterior walls from a router sitting in the front living room.

The fix is almost never a stronger camera. It is moving the HomeBase toward the middle of the house or the garage side, testing the signal at the exact mounting spot before drilling, adding a Wi-Fi extension when the test is weak, or switching that one camera to a plug-in model if the garage already has an outlet.

Winter on the North and South Shores

eufy's own support page lists an operating range of -4°F to 122°F for its battery cameras and warns that extreme cold can stop them temporarily. Long Island nights seldom reach that floor, but cold still drains batteries faster and slows charging, and users report cameras dropping offline on the coldest nights and reconnecting once the air warms. Two Island habits help. Aim solar panels for the low winter sun rather than the high summer sun. And for East End and waterfront homes that sit empty from fall to spring, plan power for months with nobody around to swap a battery, mounting cameras under sheltered eaves away from salt spray.

Where the electrical line falls in Nassau and Suffolk

Most eufy gear never touches 120-volt wiring. A doorbell on the existing button wires, a battery or solar camera and a plug-in floodlight are low-voltage jobs. The doorbell has one check that catches people out: eufy's wired doorbells call for a 16 to 24 volt AC transformer rated at 30VA, and many older chime transformers fall short. A meter test before mounting saves a second trip.

A hardwired floodlight where no outdoor box exists is different. That is line-voltage work for a licensed electrician. In the Town of Hempstead, the town code says new wiring cannot be energized for use until an approved inspection agency issues its final certificate, so in Nassau plan for the electrician and the inspection before the floodlight goes up. Suffolk is more permissive about homeowners doing their own repairs and licenses many trades at the county level. Either way, the electrician's work is quoted by the electrician and named on the camera quote, not hidden inside it. Village and HOA rules about what can hang on a front facade are worth checking too.

Privacy settings to switch on before handover

  • End-to-end encryption. Mozilla looked at the privacy of eufy's S350 indoor camera and found it supports end-to-end encryption for cloud video but does not switch it on by default. Turn it on wherever your model offers it.
  • Two-step verification on the eufy account, tied to the owner's phone, not the installer's.
  • Family sharing through app invitations instead of handing out the main password.
  • Firmware updated on the HomeBase and every camera at the end of the visit.
  • Motion zones drawn around your own driveway and walk, not the neighbor's yard or the whole street.

A short scenario: the ranch with a garage out back

Picture a slab ranch in central Nassau with the router in the front living room and a detached garage about eighty feet behind the house. The doorbell goes on the existing button after the transformer passes a meter test. The HomeBase moves from the living room to a rear bedroom. A battery camera is held against the garage fascia, signal-tested, then mounted aiming up the driveway. The garage has an outlet, so a plug-in floodlight covers the back door. No electrician is needed. Move the same plan to a Suffolk lot where the garage has no power, and a solar panel on the garage's south gable replaces the plug-in floodlight.

FAQ

Will cold weather kill my eufy battery cameras?

Usually not for good. eufy lists an operating range down to -4°F, and in very cold weather a battery camera can go offline temporarily and reconnect once it warms. Cold does shorten each charge, so solar panels should face the winter sun.

Why does my garage eufy camera's battery die so fast?

It is most likely at the edge of the HomeBase or Wi-Fi range and working hard to stay connected. Moving the HomeBase, adding a Wi-Fi extension or switching that camera to plug-in power usually fixes it.

Do I need an electrician to install a eufy floodlight on Long Island?

Not for a plug-in floodlight. A hardwired floodlight where there is no existing outdoor box needs a licensed electrician, and in Nassau the new wiring needs an inspection before it can be used.

Will you install eufy cameras I already bought?

Yes. Customer-supplied eufy equipment is installed at labor-only rates from the Nassau or Suffolk list. Send the model names first so compatibility with your HomeBase and doorbell wiring can be checked.

Is eufy video encrypted?

eufy offers end-to-end encryption for cloud video on many models, but on at least one camera a privacy review found it switched off out of the box. Turning it on is part of a careful setup.
