For most houses and ordinary shops in Nassau and Suffolk the answer is no, and Hikvision remains a capable, well-supported system. For some organizations it is yes. First: is your property one the rules rule out?

The Federal Communications Commission stopped authorizing new Hikvision equipment in late 2022, and in mid-2026 it barred importing or marketing previously authorized Hikvision gear for public safety, government facilities and national security uses, effective July 16, 2026. The same notice says people may keep using equipment they already own if it was bought legally and carries an existing authorization. Hikvision is contesting the FCC in court.

On Long Island that splits buyers into two groups.

  • Usually fine: private homes, rental houses, most retail stores, restaurants, offices and warehouses that do not do federal work.
  • Choose another brand: fire district houses, ambulance corps buildings, village halls and town offices; any company supplying a federal agency, because of the Section 889 contracting rule; and any house of worship or nonprofit spending a security grant, because federal grant rules name Hikvision as covered equipment. Nonprofit Security Grant awards reached several Nassau congregations in 2026, so this comes up more often than people expect.

Camera choices for Island conditions

Three Hikvision lines cover most Long Island spots.

  • ColorVu keeps color at night with a built-in white light, useful for a driveway on a dark Suffolk road where plate and car color matter.
  • AcuSense classifies people and vehicles, which cuts the alerts that swaying privet hedges, deer and passing headlights would otherwise send to your phone all night.
  • TandemVu pairs a fixed wide view with a zooming lens; it earns its cost on a large business lot, rarely on a house.

Near the water, from Long Beach to Montauk and along the North Shore harbors, salt air attacks cheap brackets and screws first. Ask for stainless fasteners and sealed junction boxes, and expect to clean domes more often than an inland homeowner would.

Where the recorder lives

The camera gets the attention, but the recorder decides whether you have footage after a bad night. Two Island habits cause trouble: putting the recorder on a basement floor and putting it in a hot, closed garage. South Shore homes that took water in Sandy, or lifted homes on pilings, need the recorder raised or moved upstairs. Anywhere, it should sit on a battery backup together with the PoE switch and the modem. A home-size battery covers flickers and the first hour or two of an outage, not a week like the one after Isaias, so think of it as a bridge, not a solution.

Also learn the export routine before you need it: choose a camera and time window in playback, copy the clip to a USB stick with the player the recorder offers, and check that the recorder clock matches your phone. Police will ask for exactly that file.

Extending an older Hikvision system

Many Island houses already have a Hikvision recorder from years ago. That is often a starting point rather than scrap. Cable that tests good, mounting spots and the recorder location can be reused. A sensible order is: update or replace the recorder first, change default passwords and close any forwarded ports, then add or swap cameras over time. Frequently asked questions

Is it legal to install Hikvision cameras on Long Island in 2026?

For a house or a typical shop or office, owning and using legally purchased Hikvision equipment remains allowed. The 2026 FCC restrictions target importing and marketing it for public safety, government facilities and national security uses.

Who on Long Island should not buy Hikvision?

Fire districts, ambulance corps, village and town buildings, companies that contract with federal agencies, and nonprofits or houses of worship spending federal grant money should choose another brand.

How much does Hikvision installation cost in Nassau versus Suffolk?

On the Abstract Enterprises Security Systems list, a 4–6 camera system runs $2,070–$4,025 in Nassau and $2,160–$4,200 in Suffolk, while work on a system already in place is billed at $195/hr with a 3-hour minimum.

Can you add cameras to my old Hikvision recorder?

Often, if the recorder is healthy, has free channels and its firmware can be updated. If it is too old, replacing the recorder and keeping the existing cable and cameras is a common middle path.

Where should the recorder go in a flood-prone house?

Off the basement floor, ideally upstairs in a ventilated closet, on a battery backup shared with the PoE switch and modem.
