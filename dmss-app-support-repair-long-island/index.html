Three Long Island moments that knock DMSS offline

The power went out

When a nor'easter or summer storm takes the lines down, the recorder, router and modem all lose power together. When power returns, the router may hand the recorder a new local address, the modem may come up after the recorder gives up looking for it, or the hard drive may not mount after a hard shutdown. The app then shows "device offline" even though nothing is physically broken.

The internet provider changed the router

Switching between Optimum and Verizon Fios, or getting a replacement gateway after an outage, gives the house a new network. A recorder set to a fixed (static) IP address from the old router can end up on the wrong subnet and simply vanish. On Reddit's Dahua forum, the most common one-line fix for this exact case is switching the recorder from static to DHCP and power-cycling it.

The house was sold

Plenty of Nassau and Suffolk homes come with a camera system left behind. The recorder is usually still registered to the previous owner's DMSS account, and nobody knows the admin password. That is a recovery job, not a broken system.

Inherited a Dahua system with the house?

Take a photo of the label on the recorder (model and serial number) and of the screen it shows when connected to a monitor. Dahua recorders support verified password resets that use the device details, and many can also reset through a security email or questions set at installation. Once you have admin access, remove the device from the old account, add it to a DMSS account in your name, change every password, and check that the cameras were not left on default logins. If the recorder is old enough that firmware updates stopped, that is the moment to price a new recorder that keeps the existing cameras and cable.

When alerts stop coming

Push notifications fail at three different layers, and fixing only one rarely works:

  1. The phone: battery optimization, focus modes or a revoked notification permission quietly stop DMSS from running in the background.
  2. The device in the app: under Device Details, the notification switch and the event types (motion, intrusion, tripwire) have to be enabled. Toggling it off, saving, then on again re-registers the phone.
  3. The recorder: the event rule itself must be set to push, and P2P must be on, because alerts travel over that same cloud link.

Some users on the Dahua subreddit report that deleting and recreating the DMSS account was the only thing that brought alerts back. Try the three layers first, since recreating the account means re-adding every device.

Keeping a seasonal East End system reachable all winter

A house in the Hamptons or on the North Fork that sits closed for months needs the system to recover without help. Four settings do most of the work: a weekly automatic reboot on the recorder, DHCP so it accepts whatever address the router gives after an outage, a battery backup on the recorder, modem and router, and email or app alerts for disk errors and video loss, not just motion. Check the app once a week; if it shows offline after a storm, you know to send someone before spring.

Frequently asked questions

Why does DMSS say device offline after a power outage?

Usually the recorder came back before the modem or router, received a new local address, or lost its P2P cloud registration. The cameras are often still recording to the hard drive the whole time.

My internet provider replaced my router and DMSS stopped working. What happened?

A recorder set to a static IP from the old router may now be on the wrong network. Switching it to DHCP and restarting it often brings it back online.

Can I take over a Dahua system that came with the house?

Usually, yes. A verified password reset using the recorder's details restores admin access, then you remove it from the previous owner's DMSS account and add it to yours.

Why do I see events in DMSS but get no push notifications?

Check the phone's battery and notification settings, the notification switch under Device Details in the app, and the recorder's event rules. P2P also has to be on, because alerts travel over it.

Do you fix DMSS on systems someone else installed?

Yes. Most DMSS repairs are on systems installed by another company or a previous owner.
