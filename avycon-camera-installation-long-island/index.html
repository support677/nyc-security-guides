Coax or Cat6 on a Long Island property

On a slab ranch, the attic is usually an easy path to the eaves, so pulling new Cat6 is often reasonable. On a two-story colonial where the old coax vanishes into finished walls and a basement ceiling someone drywalled, reusing it on a hybrid recorder can save a lot of opening up, as long as the cable tests clean. On a retail strip along a road like Sunrise Highway or Jericho Turnpike, the drop ceiling makes new runs simple, and IP cameras give sharper faces at the register and better detail across the lot.

We test old cable before promising anything. A hybrid recorder only pays off when the coax is sound; otherwise you end up replacing the cable later anyway.

When NDAA compliance is a real requirement

AVYCON markets an NDAA-compliant line, and for some buyers that matters. Section 889 of the 2019 National Defense Authorization Act bars federal agencies from buying video surveillance equipment from certain named manufacturers, and from contracting with companies that use it. If your business works on federal contracts, or a customer's purchasing office asks for compliant equipment, ask for the compliance statement for each exact model number on the quote, not just the brand name. For a family home, compliance is a preference rather than a rule.

Mixing AVYCON with other brands

ONVIF is the standard that lets one brand's camera talk to another brand's recorder, but support varies by firmware and by feature. Installers in online trade forums describe AVYCON cameras that would not add to a different brand's NVR until settings on the camera were changed first. Motion events, AI detection and two-way audio are the features most likely to drop out across brands. If you want to keep older cameras, we add each one to the new recorder and test recording, motion alerts and playback before the old DVR leaves the property.

What an upgrade visit looks like

  1. List every camera, cable run and the current recorder, and export any footage you need to keep.
  2. Test each cable for continuity and picture quality.
  3. Mount the new recorder somewhere ventilated and out of plain sight, such as a basement utility area or a locked office closet.
  4. Swap or reconnect cameras one channel at a time, checking each picture as it comes up.
  5. Set recording schedules, motion zones and retention so you know how many days are kept.
  6. Set up phone access through the AVYCON ONE app, giving every person their own user rather than one shared login.

Questions to ask before you upgrade

  • Which of my cables tested good, and which need replacing?

  • If I need compliance, is every model on the quote on AVYCON's NDAA-compliant list?

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep my old coax cameras with an AVYCON system?

Often, yes. AVYCON makes hybrid DVRs that accept coax cameras, so working cameras on good cable can stay. If the cable is damaged, or you want sharper IP cameras later, a phased move to Cat6 is the alternative.

Will AVYCON cameras work with a Reolink or other-brand NVR?

Sometimes, through ONVIF, but it is not guaranteed. Installers report cases where AVYCON cameras would not add to another brand's recorder until camera settings were changed. Even when they connect, motion and AI features may not carry over, so test each camera before relying on it.

Is AVYCON NDAA compliant?

AVYCON offers NDAA-compliant models. If compliance is required for a contract, check each model number against the manufacturer's documentation rather than assuming the whole catalog qualifies.

Why does the AVYCON ONE app take so long to load my cameras?

App reviewers mention slow loading. Common causes are low upload speed at the property, high-resolution main streams used for remote viewing, many cameras on one screen, or out-of-date recorder firmware and app versions. Using the lower-resolution substream for phones usually helps.

How long will an AVYCON NVR keep recordings?

It depends on drive size, the number of cameras, resolution, compression and whether recording is continuous or triggered by motion. A small home system may keep weeks; a busy business recording 4K around the clock fills a drive faster.
