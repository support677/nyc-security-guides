AVYCON Camera Installation Brooklyn calls in Brooklyn often start with a system somebody else bought. A house changes hands and the recorder in the cellar comes with it, a shop takes over a storefront with cameras already on the ceiling, or a landlord inherits a walk-up with an old coax DVR. AVYCON is a professional, wired camera line, so the questions are less about pairing and more about cable, power and passwords. This guide covers those situations, then new installs. Abstract Enterprises Security Systems lists the full service on its AVYCON Camera Installation Brooklyn page.

You bought the house. The recorder came with it.

A Brooklyn closing rarely includes a camera login. AVYCON's FAQ says its equipment leaves the factory with no default password. That is safer, but it means there is nothing to fall back on: the recorder has to be reset using AVYCON's own procedure (cameras have a separate one), and the reset wipes its settings. The order that works:

  1. Confirm the recorder powers up and shows live video on a monitor.
  2. Check the hard drive status and how far back the recordings go.
  3. Reset and set a new strong admin password, written down and kept with the house papers.
  4. Add the recorder to your own phone in the AVYCON ONE app, and remove any old user accounts.
  5. Walk every camera: dark ones usually mean a damaged cable, a failed port or a dead camera.

Count the watts before you buy the recorder

Each PoE camera draws power from the recorder or a PoE switch, and AVYCON publishes the figures. According to AVYCON's spec sheets as summarized on the Abstract Enterprises Security Systems page, the AVC-NDE81F28 8MP turret draws up to 7.1 watts on standard PoE, while the AVC-NPE81F180 panoramic turret needs the higher-power PoE+ standard and up to 12 watts. A house full of turrets fits most recorders easily. Add a panoramic yard camera, white-light deterrence and a cold night, and it is worth checking the recorder's total PoE budget or adding a switch.

Where the recorder lives in a Brooklyn building

In a rowhouse that is the cellar, on a shelf off the floor and away from the boiler and water heater, with a small battery backup. In a walk-up, a locked closet or the super's office. In a store, somewhere a thief cannot carry it out with the register. AVYCON recorders overwrite the oldest video when the drive fills, so the system keeps recording; the question is whether the drive holds enough days. The security camera storage calculator helps size it.

Running a store? Three rules before switching on

  • Face recognition. Some AVYCON cameras offer it. Under New York City rules, a business that gathers customers' biometric data, such as face prints, has to display a clear notice at each door customers use. Ordinary recording does not trigger that rule. Leave the feature off unless you have decided to use it and put up the sign.
  • Audio. Keep sound off at the counter unless you have legal advice; New York's eavesdropping law covers conversations you are not part of.
  • Funding. On October 8, 2026, the City Council announced $200,000 for small businesses to buy security technology, split among the five boroughs through their chambers of commerce; in Brooklyn that is the Brooklyn Chamber of Commerce. Eligibility rules apply, so check with the Chamber.

Frequently asked questions

We bought a house with an AVYCON system and nobody knows the password. What now?

AVYCON equipment has no default password, so the recorder is reset using AVYCON's published procedure, which clears its settings. Then set a new admin password, add it to your phone in the AVYCON ONE app and check every camera.

Can AVYCON cameras reuse the old coax in our building?

Yes, if the coax is in good shape. AVYCON HD analog cameras and a DVR can use it, which keeps finished walls closed. The published Brooklyn starting point is a four-camera HD analog package from $2,188 installed.

What app do I use to view an AVYCON system?

AVYCON's FAQ points to the AVYCON ONE app for phones. The recorder is added to the app from its system information settings.

Will the recorder stop when the hard drive is full?

No. AVYCON recorders overwrite the oldest footage. Size the drive so it holds the number of days you actually need.

Do I need a sign for AVYCON face recognition in my Brooklyn store?

If the store collects biometric identifiers from customers, NYC rules require a clear sign at every customer entrance. Normal recording without face recognition does not trigger that requirement.

What does hidden wiring cost for an AVYCON camera?

The Abstract Enterprises Security Systems Brooklyn list adds $200 per camera to hide cable inside hollow drywall, for runs up to 100 feet. Plaster and brick walls are assessed on site.
