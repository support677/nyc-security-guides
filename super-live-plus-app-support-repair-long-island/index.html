Read the icon before anything else

In the app's device list, a green icon means the phone reached the recorder, grey means it didn't, and a blinking icon means it is still trying. Note which one you see, and then turn off Wi-Fi on your phone and look again. If the cameras play on home Wi-Fi but not on cellular, the cameras and disk are almost certainly fine and the problem is how the recorder is reached from outside. That single test narrows most island repairs before anyone drives out.

The Nassau commuter: fine at home, grey on the train

In the dense villages and hamlets of Nassau, the most common story is a system that was set up while the installer stood in the living room. Somebody typed the recorder's in-house IP address into the app, which means it can only be found from inside the house. The other frequent cause is a new gateway. Optimum and Verizon Fios both replace routers during upgrades, and a new box drops any port-forwarding rules the old one held and may hand the recorder a new address.

The lasting fix is to add the recorder through its NAT or QR-code connection rather than a local or forwarded address, and to give it a reserved address on the router so the next provider visit doesn't break it again. Short Nassau drives mean this is usually a single visit.

The East End owner: nobody there for months

Out past Riverhead, many houses sit empty from fall to spring, and the person with the keys is often a caretaker. Here the questions are different. Did the recorder come back after the last PSEG Long Island outage? Is its clock still right, so that footage is searchable by date? Is it still recording, or only showing live view? A recorder can restart before the modem has internet and then never reconnect, so the order of power-up matters.

Before closing a seasonal house, leave the modem, router, recorder and any network switch powered, ideally on a small battery backup, and turn off only what doesn't feed the cameras. Some East End addresses have a single wired provider; a cellular router can serve as a backup path.

The landlord with an accessory apartment: who still has access?

Long Island has plenty of houses with an apartment over the garage or in the basement, and cameras shared with a tenant don't always get unshared when the tenant leaves. The app lets you share a device, but the cleaner approach is a separate user on the recorder for each person, with only the cameras they should see. When someone moves out, delete their user and change the admin password if it was ever handed over. Installation-day passwords that were never changed belong on the same list.

The store on a turnpike: one internet line, two jobs

Shops along Hempstead Turnpike, Jericho Turnpike and Route 58 often run the recorder and the card terminal on the same connection. Watching high-definition streams remotely uses upload bandwidth, so choose the app's lower-definition substream for routine checks and keep full quality for reviewing an incident. Owners with a Nassau store and a Suffolk store can keep both recorders in one app list, named clearly so nobody opens the wrong site.

Five things not to do while you wait

  • Don't factory-reset the recorder; it can wipe users, schedules and network settings.
  • Don't keep guessing the password; many recorders lock after failed attempts.
  • Don't delete and re-add the device until you know how it was added the first time.
  • Don't unplug the recorder mid-recording to "see if it helps"; restart the router first.
  • Don't rely on phone screenshots as evidence; export the clip from the recorder itself.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Super Live Plus show my cameras offline only when I travel abroad?

The app's server region, the Domain setting, may not match where you are. Changing it in the app's settings to the region you're in, or to a global option, usually brings the device back.

Optimum or Fios replaced our router and the app stopped working. What changed?

The new gateway has none of the old port-forwarding rules and may have given the recorder a new local address. Re-adding the recorder through its NAT connection and reserving its address prevents a repeat.

After a power outage the recorder works but the time is wrong. Does it matter?

Yes. Playback is searched by date and time, so a wrong clock makes footage hard to find and less useful as evidence. Set the time and enable automatic time sync once the internet is back.

How do I let family see the cameras without giving them my password?

Create a separate user on the recorder with view-only rights to the cameras they need, or use the app's device sharing. Remove that access the same way when it's no longer needed.

How do I get a clip off the system for the police or an insurer?

Find the moment in the app's playback, then export the original file from the recorder to a USB drive. A recording made from the phone screen loses quality and the time stamp.
